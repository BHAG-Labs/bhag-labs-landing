CONCEPT REVIEW

BHAG Labs logo, v15

Flat, 2D. The stem becomes the actual spine of the B; bowls fuse to it on the right.

What changed from v13:
  • Stem on top, not behind. The stem now visibly forms the left spine of the B. The bowls' left edges are clipped by the stem, so each bowl reads as a D fused to the spine.
  • Vertical stem. Lean dropped from 20° to 0° for maximum B-clarity. Stroke bumped slightly (14 → 16) so the spine carries proper weight.
  • Bowl alignment. Both bowls share the same left edge (x=68), anchored to the stem. Top bowl r=48, bottom bowl r=60 (preserves v13's 5:4 ratio).
  • Lens kept. Where the two bowls intersect, forest green (light) / ochre (dark) reads as the waist of the B — same product-market-fit narrative.
  • Composition centered. Mark bounding box centered on the 256×256 canvas.
  • 2D, flat. No gradients, no shadows, no 3D.
